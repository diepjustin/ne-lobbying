Read this before quoting it

  • The site truncates names in its own markup. The bill table really does publish "Associated Beverage Distributors of…" — 28% of a sample arrived cut short, and the full name appears nowhere on that page. Every row here carries the numeric principal and lobbyist IDs, and names are resolved from each principal's own detail page.
  • Rows that look identical usually are not. One lobbyist can file more than once for the same principal on the same bill; the rows differ only by registration ID. Deduplicating on the visible columns destroys real records.
  • "Neutral" is a real position, and it is common. Registering neutral on a bill is not the same as not registering.
  • Electronic filings begin in 2015. Earlier documents were not filed electronically; the Clerk provides them on request.
  • A registered position is not a vote and not an outcome. It records what an interest told the Legislature it wanted.
  • Expense figures are self-reported quarterly totals, not audited spending, and Form B (what a lobbyist received) and Form C (what a principal paid) describe the same money from two sides — adding them together double-counts it.
